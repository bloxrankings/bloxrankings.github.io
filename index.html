Blox Rankings Uploader

Blox Rankings Uploader is a small personal tool used only by the owner of the Blox Rankings YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does
  • Creates short videos that rank Roblox games using Roblox's public statistics.
  • Uploads those videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, adds English captions and adds them to the channel's playlist.
  • Schedules when the owner's videos become public.
  • Generates the narration with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech.

The tool signs in with Google only to act on the owner's own channel and Google Cloud project. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Blox Rankings is a fan channel and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
